# StyleHub Online Store
## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes approximately 3–7 business days after dispatch. Express shipping takes approximately 1–3 business days after dispatch.

### 2. How much does shipping cost?
Standard shipping costs $5.99. Express shipping costs $12.99. Orders totaling $75 or more qualify for free standard shipping.

### 3. Can I return an item?
Yes. Customers may request a return within 30 days of receiving their order. Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original condition.

### 4. How long do refunds take?
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing may take 5–10 business days after approval.

### 5. What payment methods do you accept?
StyleHub accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal.

### 6. Can I exchange an item?
Customers may contact Customer Support to ask about an exchange. Exchanges depend on product availability and must be approved by Customer Support.

### 7. How can I track my order?
Customers receive a tracking number by email after their order has been dispatched. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become available.

### 8. What should I do if my order arrives damaged?
Contact StyleHub Customer Support with your order number and photographs showing the damage. Customer Support will review the issue and explain the available next steps.

### 9. Can I cancel my order?
The current StyleHub policies do not specify an order cancellation procedure. Customers should contact Customer Support for assistance.

### 10. Do you offer international shipping?
The current StyleHub policies do not specify international shipping availability. Customers should contact Customer Support to confirm.

### 11. How can I contact customer support?
Customers can contact StyleHub Customer Support for assistance with orders, returns, payments, and product questions.
