# StyleHub Online Store
## Payment Policy

### 1. Accepted Payment Methods
StyleHub currently accepts:
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- PayPal

### 2. Payment Processing
- Payment is collected during checkout.
- Orders are processed after payment authorization is successful.
- Customers receive an order confirmation email after a successful purchase.

### 3. Payment Failures
- If a payment is declined, customers should check their payment details.
- Customers may contact their bank or payment provider for assistance.
- Customers may try another accepted payment method.
- A declined payment does not guarantee that an order has been placed.

### 4. Duplicate Charges
- If a customer believes they have been charged more than once, they should contact StyleHub Customer Support.
- Customers should provide their order number and relevant payment details.
- StyleHub will review the transaction information.

### 5. Refund Payments
- Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
- Refund processing may take 5–10 business days after approval.
- The time it takes for funds to appear depends on the customer's bank or payment provider.

### 6. Payment Security
- Customers should only enter payment information through the official StyleHub checkout.
- Customers should never share their full card number, security code, or account password with Customer Support.
- StyleHub Customer Support will never ask customers to disclose their account passwords.

### 7. Important Information
- Payment authorization does not always mean an order has been successfully placed.
- Payment disputes and suspected duplicate charges must be reviewed by Customer Support.
