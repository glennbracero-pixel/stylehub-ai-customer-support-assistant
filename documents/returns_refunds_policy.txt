# StyleHub Online Store
## Returns and Refunds Policy

### 1. Return Eligibility
- Customers may request a return within 30 days of receiving their order.
- Items must be unused, unworn, and in their original condition.
- Items must include their original tags and packaging.
- Proof of purchase is required.

### 2. Non-Returnable Items
- Items marked as final sale cannot be returned.
- Gift cards are non-refundable.
- Items damaged through customer misuse are not eligible for return.

### 3. How to Request a Return
- Customers should contact StyleHub Customer Support to request a return.
- Customers must provide their order number and reason for the return.
- Customer Support will review the request and provide return instructions.
- Customers should not ship items back before receiving return instructions.

### 4. Refund Processing
- Refunds are reviewed after the returned item is received and inspected.
- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Refund processing may take 5–10 business days after approval.
- The time it takes for funds to appear depends on the customer's bank or payment provider.

### 5. Exchanges
- Customers may contact Customer Support to ask about exchanging an eligible item.
- Exchanges depend on product availability.
- Customer Support must confirm exchange eligibility before the item is returned.

### 6. Damaged or Incorrect Items
- Customers should contact Customer Support if they receive a damaged or incorrect item.
- Customers should provide their order number and photographs showing the issue.
- StyleHub will review the information and explain the available next steps.

### 7. Important Information
- A return request does not guarantee approval.
- Refunds are not guaranteed until the returned item has been inspected.
- Customer Support must review exceptions on a case-by-case basis.
