# StyleHub Online Store
## Shipping Policy

### 1. Order Processing
- Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.
- Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.
- Customers receive an order confirmation email after placing an order.

### 2. Shipping Times
- Standard shipping takes approximately 3–7 business days after dispatch.
- Express shipping takes approximately 1–3 business days after dispatch.
- Delivery times are estimates and may vary depending on the destination and courier.

### 3. Shipping Fees
- Standard shipping costs $5.99.
- Express shipping costs $12.99.
- Orders totaling $75 or more qualify for free standard shipping.

### 4. Tracking Orders
- Customers receive a tracking number by email after their order has been dispatched.
- Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become available.
- Customers can use their tracking number on the courier's website.

### 5. Delayed Deliveries
- If an order has not arrived within the estimated delivery period, customers should contact StyleHub Customer Support.
- StyleHub will review the shipment information and assist the customer.

### 6. Important Information
- Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.
- Customs delays and unexpected courier disruptions may affect delivery.
- StyleHub does not guarantee delivery on a specific date.
